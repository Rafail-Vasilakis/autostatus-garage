# Board Verdict: AutoStatus (GarageBot)

**The Vote: 1 FUND, 2 FUND IF (0 PASS) | Average Score: 8.0 / 10**

---

## 1. Top Risks Raised Across Multiple Members
These risks were flagged independently by more than one board member and represent the primary existential threats to the business:

1. **Behavioral Inertia & Non-Tech Mechanic Abandonment:** Mechanics operate in fast, physically exhausting environments. If entering a vehicle takes more than 15 seconds, or if tapping status updates feels like administrative homework during a busy morning, the tool will be abandoned within 2 weeks of launch.
2. **The Distribution Bottleneck:** Traditional digital marketing (ads, SEO, cold emails) will fail completely in this market. Independent mechanics do not browse software forums or check LinkedIn. Acquisition must rely on direct, in-person walk-ins or piggybacking on existing distribution networks (e.g. daily auto parts delivery vans).
3. **Messaging Cost Erosion & Feature Creep:** Uncapped Viber/WhatsApp Business API fees could erode the €49/mo subscription margin if high-volume shops send hundreds of unmetered updates. Simultaneously, customer requests for accounting/ERP features could bloat the product and destroy its core speed advantage.

---

## 2. Master Condition Checklist
Before scaling or spending capital, the founder must satisfy these conditions:

- [ ] **Garage Zero 14-Day Pilot:** Deploy the prototype in the founder's father's repair shop for 14 continuous days. Document that customer status inquiry calls drop by at least 50% and that the mechanic logs every incoming car without friction.
- [ ] **The 15-Second Intake Threshold:** Benchmark car intake time. License Plate + Phone + Name must take under 15 seconds (using license plate camera/OCR where possible).
- [ ] **API Unit Economics & Cost Guardrails:** Cap and structure WhatsApp/Viber messaging costs per garage so gross margin stays firmly above 80% at €49/month (using SMS fallbacks and message bundling).
- [ ] **5 In-Person Sales Closes:** The founder must physically visit 10 neighboring independent auto repair shops and sign up at least 5 paying shops on a 30-day risk-free pilot.
- [ ] **Automated 11-Month Retention Engine:** Build the automated 11-month service and 24-month KTEO reminder module into the pilot to create permanent switching costs.

---

## 3. The Strongest Version of This Business
The board's consensus is that this should **never** be marketed as an "auto repair management software" or an "ERP". The strongest version of this company is **The Transparent Garage Network**: a specialized B2B micro-SaaS that sells one specific, irresistible outcome: *"Eliminate 70% of phone interruptions, double customer trust with photo-proof repair approvals, and automatically recall past customers for their annual service."* 

By dominating a dense, localized geographic cluster of independent repair shops (leveraging local auto parts distributors for warm referrals), AutoStatus creates an insurmountable local monopoly long before legacy ERP vendors realize what happened.
