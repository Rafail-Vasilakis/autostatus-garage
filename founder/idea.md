# AutoStatus (GarageBot)

- What it is: A lightweight mobile web tool for independent auto repair shops that eliminates daily phone interruptions by automating customer repair status updates, photo-based damage approvals, and annual service/KTEO reminders via Viber and WhatsApp.
- Who it is for: Independent auto repair shop owners and mechanics (initially independent workshops in Greece, expanding regionally).
- What it sells, at what price: B2B SaaS subscription at €49/month (or €490/year) per workshop, with a 30-day free trial. Includes unlimited automated Viber/WhatsApp status messages, photo approval links, and scheduled annual service/KTEO reminders.
- Where and how: B2B direct sales and word-of-mouth. Cloud-hosted mobile PWA (zero installation, runs in browser with large oil-friendly buttons).
- Budget and constraints: Solo software engineer (strong Python/Flask/AI stack), direct daily access to a functioning pilot garage (father's auto repair shop) for testing. Minimal initial budget (<€200 for SMS/Viber API credits and basic hosting).
