# Offers lens
Score: 8/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Acute, daily pain in an affluent market:** Car repair shops generate €5,000–€30,000+ monthly in revenue. Losing 1–2 hours every day to telephone tag, dirty-hand phone calls, and cars stalled on hydraulic lifts costs them hundreds of euros weekly. €49/month is less than the billable labor rate of a single hour.
- **Flawless Value Equation:**
  - *Dream Outcome:* Reclaiming 10+ hours a week, zero phone interruptions during delicate mechanical work, and faster bay turnaround.
  - *Perceived Likelihood:* Very high because the mechanic immediately sees the Viber message land on their own phone during onboarding.
  - *Time Delay:* Immediate (first car logged gets an update in seconds).
  - *Effort & Sacrifice:* Minimal—reduced from a 5-minute phone call to a 2-second tap.
- **Immediate Trust & Upsell Mechanism:** The photo-based damage approval transforms skeptical car owners into approving buyers. Car owners historically suspect mechanics of inventing repairs; a high-res photo sent directly to Viber with an itemized price dismantles buyer skepticism.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Behavioral inertia and habit abandonment:** Mechanics are creatures of manual routine. If entering a car takes more than 15 seconds, or if the mechanic forgets to tap status updates during a hectic morning rush, the tool becomes shelfware.
2. **Uncapped messaging costs destroying margins:** Viber Business and WhatsApp Business API charge per business-initiated conversation (~€0.03–€0.05 per conversation). If a busy garage services 200 cars a month with multiple status updates, messaging costs could consume 30–50% of the €49/mo fee if not structured with SMS/Viber caps or local gateway routing.
3. **Selling as "Software" instead of an "Outcome":** If pitched as "SaaS for garages", mechanics will say "I already have software" or "I'm not a computer guy". It must be sold as "The Anti-Distraction & Fast-Approval System that puts €500 extra in your pocket this month."

## The questions I would ask the founder
1. How many cars does your father's garage process per week, and exactly how many minutes per day does he spend on the phone with customers?
2. What happens to the pricing when WhatsApp or Viber increases API conversation fees—is the cost passed through or absorbed?
3. Could you offer a "30-day Free Trial + We set up your first 50 customers" offer so the mechanic has zero financial or technical friction to start?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Pilot Proof in Garage Zero:** The founder must deploy the tool in their father's shop for 14 continuous days and prove in numbers that incoming customer inquiry calls drop by at least 50%.
2. **Strict WhatsApp/Viber Cost Guardrails:** Message deliverability costs per shop must be benchmarked and capped so gross margins stay above 80% at €49/month.
3. **Risk-Free Onboarding Guarantee:** The offer must include a 30-day risk-free pilot: "If it doesn't save you at least 5 hours of phone time in your first 30 days, you pay nothing."
