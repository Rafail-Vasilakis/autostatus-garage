# Board Brief: AutoStatus (GarageBot)

## The Core Pitch
AutoStatus is a dedicated mobile workflow tool built for independent car mechanics and auto repair shops (συνεργεία αυτοκινήτων). It addresses the single largest daily operational bottleneck in an auto shop: mechanics stopping manual work with greasy hands 20-40 times a day to answer phone calls ("Is my car ready?", "How much will it cost?"), and having cars stuck on lifts waiting for customer phone approval on extra repairs.

## How It Works
1. **Intake in 15 seconds:** When a car arrives, the mechanic (or assistant) enters License Plate, Owner Name, Mobile Number, and expected pickup time into a mobile web app designed with large touch targets.
2. **One-Tap Status Automation:** 
   - [On Lift / Being Inspected]
   - [Waiting for Parts]
   - [Ready for Pickup]
   Every tap triggers an instant, branded Viber/WhatsApp message to the vehicle owner with status and invoice total.
3. **Photo-based Damage Approval:** If the mechanic discovers extra damage (e.g. worn brake discs, broken water pump), they take a 5-second photo on their smartphone, enter the price, and tap send. The customer receives an instant Viber link: "We found worn brake discs. Additional cost: €85. [Approve Repair] / [Decline]". The approval is timestamped and logged.
4. **Automated Annual Retention:** At 11 months post-service or 24 months post-KTEO, the system automatically triggers an automated Viber reminder offering a 1-click booking slot for annual maintenance.

## The Business Model & Market
- **Target Audience:** Independent auto repair shops (Greece has ~15,000+ independent workshops, heavily reliant on Viber/WhatsApp).
- **Pricing:** €49/month or €490/year per shop.
- **Competitors:** Legacy desktop garage ERPs (bulky, expensive at €1,000+, not mobile-friendly, don't do customer WhatsApp/Viber messaging).
- **Founder Context:** Software engineer with Python/Flask/APIs background, whose father owns an active auto repair shop serving as the ground-zero testbed and immediate reference case.
